Common Mental Health Problems

Depression

Symptoms: Persistent sadness, loss of interest, fatigue, sleep problems.

Cure/Help: Therapy, medication, regular exercise, social support.

Anxiety

Symptoms: Excessive worry, restlessness, rapid heartbeat, sweating.

Cure/Help: Breathing exercises, therapy, mindfulness, healthy lifestyle.

Stress

Symptoms: Irritability, tension, headaches, difficulty concentrating.

Cure/Help: Relaxation techniques, time management, exercise, support groups.

PTSD

Symptoms: Flashbacks, nightmares, severe anxiety, uncontrollable thoughts.

Cure/Help: Therapy (CBT/EMDR), medication, support groups, relaxation.

Bipolar Disorder

Symptoms: Extreme mood swings, manic episodes, depression phases.

Cure/Help: Medication, therapy, stable routine, lifestyle management.

OCD

Symptoms: Unwanted thoughts, compulsive behaviors, anxiety relief rituals.

Cure/Help: Exposure therapy, medication, mindfulness, structured routine.
